About the Artist

I'm Mohammad Iftequaruddin, an AI & Data Science engineer based in Hyderabad, India, with a B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science from Methodist College of Engineering and Technology, affiliated with Osmania University.

Digital art and 3D creation are personal hobbies and creative interests of mine. I enjoy exploring stylized visual assets, digital imaging, visual storytelling, and experimenting with different 3D workflows and artistic techniques.

My current creative interests include stylized 3D modeling, low-poly, voxel and handcrafted styles, texture and material creation, topology, UV mapping, lighting, shading, rendering, NPR workflows, and asset optimization. I am continuously exploring these areas through personal learning and experimentation.
